Try the same three things in each

Drag the dock by its left grip and throw it — it carries your velocity and can be caught mid-flight. Press Go live and watch the tally. Press and hold anything — feedback fires on pointer-down, never on release.

The interactions are identical in all three. Only the material changes, which is the honest way to judge a direction: if one feels better, that is the material talking, not the motion.
